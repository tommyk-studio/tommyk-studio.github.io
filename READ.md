# Tommy_K Studio

A personal creative platform showcasing art, sneakers, style & culture.

## Live Site
[tommyk.gt.tc](http://tommyk.gt.tc)

## About
Tommy_k Studio is built for people who express thier personality through visuals. No caption. No explanation. Just art, fashion & style that speaks for itself.

## Pages
- **Home** - hero landing page with preview cards
- **Gallery** - original digital art and Canva work
- **Sneakers** - curated sneaker collection of 32 pairs
- **Style** - outfit and everyday vibes
- **Videos** - room tours and visual showcases
- **About** - the story behind the studio
- **Contact** - collaboration and feature enquires
